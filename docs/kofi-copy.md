## Public introduction post

Title: An open workbench: knowledge, music, and creative tools

Hi, I’m Thomas. I’m bringing the different parts of my work together here: tools for exploring shared knowledge, making music, and building toward a game.

If you’re new, here are three places to start:

- **Geo Companion** is an independent browser app alongside Geo. It explores educational evidence and connected ideas, with an early Indianapolis outreach pilot. Coverage and verification still have limits. Explore: https://athsrueas.site/projects/geo-companion/
- **Way Music** has grown from a procedural score experiment into a music-studio prototype. Play and record parts, shape instruments, and explore how arrangements can respond to a game. The public repository has local run instructions: https://athsrueas.site/projects/way-music-lab/
- **Folio Studio** is a local portfolio editor for artists, with a static website build and optional Cloudflare Pages publishing: https://athsrueas.site/projects/folio-studio/

My background is in teaching mathematics. I’m interested in tools that give people room to explore and make something of their own. The project pages explain what works today and what is still a prototype.

You can follow this page for project updates and working notes. If you find something useful, tips support the time I put into research, development, testing, and explaining the work. Paying is optional; trying a tool, sharing it with someone who could use it, or telling me where you got stuck helps too.
